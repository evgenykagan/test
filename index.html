The decision tree

How your order is chosen

For each order of 100, 200, or 300 cases, consider three possible demand outcomes. You sell the smaller of demand and your order. You pay for every case you order; unsold cases are lost.

Profit = min(order, demand) × price − order × cost

Multiply each scenario’s profit by its probability, then add the three results. The order with the highest expected profit is recommended. Expected profit is a probability-weighted average, not a guarantee for any single week.
